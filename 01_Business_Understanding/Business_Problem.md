# Business Problem

## 1. Company Background

AtliQ Mart is a growing Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)
manufacturer headquartered in Gujarat, India.

The company currently operates in three cities:

- Ahmedabad
- Surat
- Vadodara

Management plans to expand the business into additional
metro and Tier-1 cities within the next two years.

---

## 2. Current Business Situation

AtliQ Mart has experienced service-level issues with some
of its key business customers.

A few customers have not renewed their annual contracts.

Management suspects that repeated delivery-service failures
may have contributed to customer dissatisfaction.

The suspected issues include:

- Orders being delivered later than the agreed delivery date.
- Orders being delivered with quantities lower than the
  quantities ordered.
- Orders being both late and incomplete over a continued
  period.

These service failures can affect the customer's ability
to maintain inventory and serve their own consumers.

---

## 3. Business Impact

Poor delivery performance can potentially result in:

1. Customer dissatisfaction
2. Contract non-renewal
3. Loss of customer business
4. Revenue risk
5. Reduced customer retention
6. Operational inefficiencies
7. Increased risk during future geographic expansion

---

## 4. Management Concern

Before expanding into additional cities, management wants
to understand whether the existing supply chain is capable
of consistently meeting customer service expectations.

The supply chain team therefore needs a mechanism to monitor
delivery performance and identify service-level failures
quickly.

---

## 5. Core Business Problem

The core problem can be summarized as:

> AtliQ Mart needs to identify where and why customer orders
> are failing to meet expected delivery service levels so
> that management can take corrective action and reduce
> the risk of customer loss before expanding into new markets.

---

## 6. Service-Level Dimensions

The analysis will focus on three primary service-level
dimensions:

### On-Time Delivery (OT)

Measures whether an order was delivered by the agreed
delivery date.

### In-Full Delivery (IF)

Measures whether the complete quantity ordered was delivered.

### On-Time In-Full (OTIF)

Measures whether the complete order was delivered on or
before the agreed delivery date.

OTIF combines both service requirements:

    On Time + In Full = OTIF

---

## 7. Analytical Scope

The analysis will investigate service performance across:

- Customers
- Cities
- Products
- Product categories
- Orders
- Order lines
- Time periods

Performance will also be compared against customer-specific
service-level targets where available.

---

## 8. Important Analytical Limitation

The available dataset will determine the extent to which the
business problem can be investigated.

If contract renewal outcomes, revenue, inventory, warehouse,
supplier, transportation, or delay-reason information is not
available, the analysis will not claim direct causation.

Instead, the project will clearly distinguish between:

- Observed facts
- Derived insights
- Hypotheses
- Recommendations
- Data limitations

This ensures that conclusions remain evidence-based.

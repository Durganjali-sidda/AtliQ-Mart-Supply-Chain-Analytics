# Business Questions

## 1. Executive-Level Questions

### Q1. What is the overall service-level performance?

Measure:

- OT %
- IF %
- OTIF %

Purpose:

Understand the overall health of the supply chain.

---

### Q2. Are we meeting customer service-level targets?

Compare:

- Actual OT vs OT Target
- Actual IF vs IF Target
- Actual OTIF vs OTIF Target

Purpose:

Identify the overall target gap.

---

### Q3. Is service performance improving or deteriorating?

Analyze OT, IF, and OTIF over time.

Purpose:

Identify persistent deterioration rather than isolated
performance issues.

---

# 2. Customer Analysis

### Q4. Which customers have the lowest OTIF performance?

Purpose:

Identify customers experiencing the poorest service.

---

### Q5. Which customers are below their OTIF targets?

Purpose:

Identify customers that are not receiving the service level
expected under their customer-specific requirements.

---

### Q6. Which customers have persistent service-level failures?

Purpose:

Distinguish repeated problems from one-time failures.

---

### Q7. Which customers should management prioritize?

Prioritization should consider available measures such as:

- OTIF gap
- Failure frequency
- Order volume
- Customer importance where available

Purpose:

Focus management attention on the most important problems.

---

# 3. Product Analysis

### Q8. Which products have the lowest In-Full performance?

Purpose:

Identify products associated with incomplete deliveries.

---

### Q9. Which products contribute the most to incomplete
deliveries?

Purpose:

Determine whether service failures are concentrated in
specific SKUs.

---

### Q10. Are particular product categories experiencing
higher service failures?

Purpose:

Identify category-level patterns.

---

# 4. Geographic Analysis

### Q11. Which cities have the weakest service performance?

Analyze:

- OT
- IF
- OTIF

Purpose:

Identify geographic concentration of service issues.

---

### Q12. Are service-level problems concentrated among specific
customers within a city?

Purpose:

Determine whether the problem is city-wide or customer-specific.

---

# 5. Time Analysis

### Q13. Which weeks or months have the highest service
failures?

Purpose:

Identify periods of operational stress.

---

### Q14. Is there a sustained decline in service performance?

Purpose:

Identify emerging problems early.

---

### Q15. Are certain products or customers repeatedly failing
during specific periods?

Purpose:

Identify recurring patterns.

---

# 6. Root-Cause Investigation

### Q16. Are OT failures or IF failures the larger contributor
to poor OTIF?

Purpose:

Determine whether the primary service problem is timing,
quantity, or both.

---

### Q17. Which products are associated with incomplete orders?

Purpose:

Investigate potential product-level drivers of IF failure.

---

### Q18. Which customers experience both frequent late and
incomplete deliveries?

Purpose:

Identify the most severe service problems.

---

# 7. Business Impact Questions

### Q19. What proportion of orders are associated with
below-target service performance?

Purpose:

Quantify the scale of the problem.

---

### Q20. What proportion of order quantity is affected by
incomplete deliveries?

Purpose:

Quantify the quantity-level exposure.

---

### Q21. Which problem areas should management address first?

Purpose:

Convert analysis into prioritization.

---

# 8. Data Gap Questions

### Q22. Do we have enough data to determine why orders are
late?

### Q23. Do we have enough data to determine why orders are
incomplete?

### Q24. Can the available data directly prove that poor
service caused contract non-renewal?

### Q25. What additional data would be required for true
root-cause analysis?

Potential additional data may include:

- Inventory
- Warehouse
- Supplier
- Transportation
- Dispatch
- Contract renewal
- Customer revenue
- Delay reason
- Stock-out reason

---

# Decision Question

The overall question connecting the analysis is:

> Which customers, products, locations, and service-level
> issues should AtliQ Mart prioritize to improve customer
> service and reduce the risk of customer loss before
> geographic expansion?

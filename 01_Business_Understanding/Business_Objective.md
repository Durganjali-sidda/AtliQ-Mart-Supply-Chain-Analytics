# Business Objective

## 1. Primary Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze AtliQ
Mart's supply chain service-level performance and identify
customers, products, cities, and time periods where delivery
performance is below expectations.

The analysis will support management in prioritizing
corrective actions before expanding into additional cities.

---

## 2. Analytical Objectives

### Objective 1 — Measure Service Performance

Calculate and monitor:

- On-Time Delivery (OT %)
- In-Full Delivery (IF %)
- On-Time In-Full (OTIF %)

---

### Objective 2 — Compare Actual Performance Against Targets

Evaluate actual service performance against the
customer-specific targets for:

- OT
- IF
- OTIF

The analysis will identify the size of the performance gap.

Example:

    Actual OTIF = 72%
    Target OTIF = 80%

    OTIF Gap = -8 percentage points

---

### Objective 3 — Identify High-Risk Customers

Identify customers that:

- Consistently perform below target.
- Have significant service-level gaps.
- Experience repeated delivery failures.

Where the available data supports it, customer importance
will also be considered when prioritizing intervention.

---

### Objective 4 — Identify Product-Level Problems

Analyze product and category performance to determine:

- Which products experience frequent incomplete deliveries.
- Which products contribute significantly to service failures.
- Whether service failures are concentrated among a small
  number of products.

---

### Objective 5 — Identify Geographic and Time-Based Patterns

Analyze performance by:

- City
- Customer
- Week
- Month

The objective is to determine whether service failures are
localized or persistent over time.

---

### Objective 6 — Prioritize Business Actions

Convert analytical findings into a prioritized action plan
for management.

The recommendations should identify:

- What needs attention?
- Why does it matter?
- Who should own the action?
- What should be investigated next?
- What additional data may be required?

---

## 3. Expected Business Outcome

The expected outcome is a decision-support solution that
allows AtliQ Mart management to:

1. Monitor supply chain service levels.
2. Identify customers requiring attention.
3. Identify products and locations contributing to service
   failures.
4. Understand performance gaps against targets.
5. Prioritize corrective actions.
6. Identify additional data required for deeper root-cause
   analysis.

---

## 4. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it can:

- Reliably calculate OT, IF, and OTIF.
- Compare actual performance with customer targets.
- Identify meaningful service-level gaps.
- Prioritize problem areas.
- Quantify business exposure where the data permits.
- Provide evidence-backed recommendations.
- Communicate findings clearly to management.

# Stakeholder Requirements

## 1. Primary Stakeholders

### Supply Chain Management

Primary interest:

- Delivery performance
- Service-level failures
- Customer-level performance
- Product-level issues
- Operational priorities

Key decisions:

- Where should operational attention be focused?
- Which customers require immediate intervention?
- Which products need investigation?

---

### Senior Management / Executive Team

Primary interest:

- Overall service-level health
- Customer risk
- Business exposure
- Performance trends
- Strategic priorities

Key decisions:

- Is the supply chain ready for expansion?
- Which problems require immediate investment?
- Which customer relationships require attention?

---

### Customer / Account Management Team

Primary interest:

- Customer-specific service performance
- Target gaps
- Persistent failures

Key decisions:

- Which customers should be contacted?
- Which customer relationships require service recovery?

---

### Data / Analytics Team

Primary interest:

- Reliable KPI calculation
- Data quality
- Consistent definitions
- Repeatable reporting

Responsibilities:

- Data validation
- KPI development
- Analysis
- Dashboard development
- Insight generation

---

# 2. Stakeholder Requirements

## Requirement 1 — Daily Service Monitoring

Management needs visibility into:

- On-Time %
- In-Full %
- OTIF %

Performance should be available at appropriate levels of
detail.

---

## Requirement 2 — Target Comparison

The solution should compare actual service performance
against customer-specific targets.

Required comparisons:

- Actual OT vs OT Target
- Actual IF vs IF Target
- Actual OTIF vs OTIF Target

---

## Requirement 3 — Customer-Level Visibility

Management should be able to identify:

- Poor-performing customers
- Customers below target
- Persistent service failures

---

## Requirement 4 — Product-Level Visibility

The solution should allow users to identify:

- Poor-performing products
- Products contributing to incomplete deliveries
- Category-level patterns

---

## Requirement 5 — Geographic Visibility

Performance should be analyzable by city.

---

## Requirement 6 — Trend Analysis

Users should be able to evaluate service performance over
time.

---

## Requirement 7 — Exception Identification

The solution should make significant performance gaps easy
to identify.

Examples:

- OTIF below target
- Persistent customer failures
- Significant product-level failures

---

# 3. Dashboard Requirements

The final Power BI solution should provide:

### Executive Overview

- OT %
- IF %
- OTIF %
- Target comparison
- Trends
- City performance

### Customer Analysis

- Customer OT/IF/OTIF
- Target gap
- Persistent performance
- Priority customers

### Product / Operations Analysis

- Product performance
- Category performance
- Quantity ordered vs delivered
- Service failures

---

# 4. Business Analyst Requirements

Beyond the requested dashboard, the analysis should:

1. Identify meaningful patterns not explicitly requested.
2. Investigate possible drivers of poor performance.
3. Quantify the scale of important problems where possible.
4. Prioritize problems based on business relevance.
5. Clearly distinguish facts from hypotheses.
6. Identify data gaps preventing deeper root-cause analysis.
7. Provide actionable recommendations.

---

# 5. Expected Deliverables

The project will produce:

1. Data analysis and validation
2. KPI definitions
3. Power BI dashboard
4. Business insights
5. Root-cause analysis
6. Customer prioritization
7. Business impact assessment
8. Recommendations
9. Executive presentation
10. Project documentation

---

# 6. Stakeholder Decision Framework

The final solution should help management answer:

> WHAT is happening?

↓

> WHERE is it happening?

↓

> WHO is affected?

↓

> WHY might it be happening?

↓

> HOW IMPORTANT is the problem?

↓

> WHAT should we do?

This decision framework will guide the entire project.
